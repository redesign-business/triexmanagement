### Services & Guarantees

- **5-Point Tenant Background Check** — Tenants are screened for five criteria before approval: credit score, gross income-rent ratio, criminal background, collections, and prior rental history.
- **Thorough Move-In/Move-Out Documentation** — Takes dozens of pictures at move-in/move-out to protect landlords' right to charge for damages.
- **Internet Advertising and Marketing, Active Prospect Follow Up** — Lists vacant properties on over 25 websites including Realtor.com, Apartments.com, and Trulia; follows up with prospects every morning or within 24 hours of receiving a guest card to shorten vacancy times and increase owner income.
- **Consistent Owner Reporting** — Accounting department works on a consistent reporting close schedule, so owners know when reports arrive.
- **Technology Capabilities** — Owner reporting done electronically via the owner portal, with archived reports and documents, and owner distributions sent via ACH.
- **Year-End 1099 Preparation** — Issues 1099s to clients for rental income per IRS regulations, accompanied by a year-end cash flow statement, and issues 1099s for paid vendors to ensure valid deductions.
- **24/7 On-Call Emergency Maintenance** — Around-the-clock emergency maintenance services for plumbing leaks and other urgent issues.
- **Online Rent Payment and Maintenance Requests** — Tenants can pay rent online and submit maintenance requests, increasing tenant satisfaction and reducing turnover.
- **Local Vendor Access** — Long-standing relationships with contractors, subcontractors, and vendors built since the mid-1980s, providing clients preferred pricing and preferred scheduling.

### Owner Portal

- Owner portal makes property information accessible from anywhere in the world with an internet connection, 24/7, and is optimized for mobile.
- Statements page archives owner packets including owner statement and bills for the reporting period.
- Transactions page allows viewing owner distributions and contributions, and making contributions free of charge for capital improvements or large repair bills.
- Documents page archives important property documents such as tenant lease, management agreement, correspondence, and 1099s for easy analysis, refinance, or tax preparation.

### Tenant Portal

- Tenant portal allows paying rent and other charges online, viewing current balance, and submitting maintenance requests.
- Supports automatic payments, automatic reminders for rent, and review of payment history.
- Provides helpful documents including the 30 Day Notice to Vacate, Release of Co-Tenant Form, Rental Terms/Criteria/Conditions, pet screening link, and draft lease template.
- Applications run on a first come, first serve basis; incomplete applications are held for 90 days without repaying the application fee.
- Rent can be paid online via e-check with zero fees.
- Security deposit refunds processed per NRS.118A, postmarked within 30 days of move-out.

# Services

Property Management is Peace of Mind

# Professional Property Management Services

* * *

### as a boutique property management firm, TriEx Management exclusively manages single family homes and Apartments under 75 units. We value the owners that we work with and are selective about the properties that we manage.

## Tenant/Leasing Services

* * *

### 5-Point Tenant Background Check

Our tenants are screened for five criteria before they may be approved to live in one of our rentals. These criteria are:

1.  Credit Score
    
2.  Gross Income-Rent Ratio
    
3.  Criminal Background
    
4.  Collections
    
5.  Prior Rental History
    

* * *

### Thorough Move-In/Move-Out Documentation

As courts continue to become more tenant friendly, the only way to protect the right of the Landlord to charge for damages at tenant move-out is thorough documentation of property conditions. TriEx Management strives to take dozens of pictures at move-in/move-out to ensure that there is no gray areas with respect to how a unit was treated during a tenancy.

* * *

### internet advertising and marketing, Active Prospect Follow Up

In order to cast a large net of exposure for our rentals, TriEx Management lists vacant properties on over 25 websites including: Realtor.com, Apartments.com, Trulia, and many more. By request and for an additional fee, we can setup rentals to premium listing sites such as apartment guide through our local sales representative.

Once the unit is posted, we follow up with prospects every morning, or within 24 hours of the guest card being received to help get an application. This active prospect follow-up leads to shorter vacancy times and more income for the owner.

* * *

## Owner/Management Services

* * *

### Consistent owner reporting

Similar to large corporate accounting offices, our accounting department works on a consistent reporting close schedule which means there is no guessing about when the report arrives.

* * *

### Technology Capabilities

All of our owner reporting is done electronically via the owner portal. The owner portal also contains archive reports and other important documents. Finally, we send owner distributions via ACH - no need to wait for the mail to arrive with your owner distribution check.

* * *

### year end 1099 preparation

Pursuant to IRS regulations, TriEx Management issues 1099s to our clients for the rental income they received during the tax year. The Form 1099 will be accompanied by a year end cash flow statement.

Additionally, we issue 1099s for vendors who were paid by TriEx Management during the tax year to ensure deductions are valid consistent with IRS regulations.

* * *

## Accounting/Maintenance Services

* * *

### 24/7 on-call emergency maintenance

Plumbing leaks and other emergencies often happen during the most inconvenient times - as a result, to give owners peace of mind and provide first-rate service to our tenants, we have 24/7 on-call emergency maintenance services.

* * *

### online rent payment and MAINTENANCE requests

Tenants of TriEx Management can pay their rent online as well as submit online maintenance requests. This service helps increase tenant satisfaction which helps reduce turnover.

* * *

### Local Vendor Access

As we have been in business since the mid-1980’s we have long standing relationships with contractors, subcontractors, and other vendors. As a result, TriEx Management clients receive preferred pricing and most importantly, preferred scheduling.

# About the Owner Portal

[Owner Portal Login](https://actprop.appfolio.com/oportal/users/log_in?a=cw&utm_source=apmsites_v2&utm_campaign=oportal_login)

## Highlighted Features:

**The owner portal makes your property information accessible from anywhere in the world with an internet connection, 24/7. It is also optimized for mobile which means you can review your important documents while on the go and not worry because TriEx Management has it handled.**

### Statements

On the statements page you will find archived owner packets. The owner packet includes the documents you requested as part of the packet (generally owner statement and bills for the reporting period).

### Transactions

The Transactions page allows you to view your owner distributions and contributions as well as make contributions free of charge to the property. This feature makes it easy to contribute money to the property if you are making a capital improvement or have a large repair bill.

### Documents

The Documents page archives important documents related to the property that you requested be shared there. Examples of this include: tenant lease, management agreement, correspondence, 1099s, etc. The documents being archived in a central location makes doing any analysis, refinance, or tax preparation quite easy and painless.

**Accessibility on TriExManagement.com**

**TriExManagement.com** makes UserWay's [Web Accessibility](https://userway.org/) Widget available which is powered by a dedicated accessibility server. The software allows **TriExManagement.com** to improve its compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1).

**Enabling the Accessibility Menu**

The **TriExManagement.com** accessibility menu can be enabled by clicking the accessibility menu icon that appears on the corner of the page. After triggering the accessibility menu, please wait a moment for the accessibility menu to load in its entirety.

**Disclaimer**

**TriEx Management** continues its efforts to constantly improve the accessibility of its site and services in the belief that it is our collective obligation to allow seamless, accessible, and unhindered use also for those of us with disabilities.  
Despite our efforts to make all pages and content on **TriExManagement.com** fully accessible, some content may not have yet been fully adapted to the strictest accessibility standards. This may be a result of not having found or identified the most appropriate technological solution.

If you are experiencing difficulty with any content on **TriExManagement.com** or require assistance with any part of our site, please contact us during normal business hours as detailed below and we will be happy to assist.

# Tenant FAQ

### How do I apply for a unit?

After you read our Rental Terms, Criteria, and Conditions, click [here](/vacancies) to be rerouted to our vacancies tab. There you can browse our current vacancies and apply for a unit directly from the page of the vacant unit.

### Why is there an application fee?

The application fee covers the cost of running the background and credit check charged by our vendor as well as office administrative cost.

### What if i apply for a unit but someone else qualifies before i can provide the necessary documentation?

As we run applications on a first come, first serve basis, whoever has the first 100% completed application will be ran. In case your application is not completed, we will hold your application for 90-Days so that you can apply for another unit without having to repay the application fee.

### Why is there a charge to pay rent via credit/debit card?

The fee for using a credit/debit card is the fee charged by the payment processing vendor for processing the credit/debit card transaction.

### Is there a way to pay my rent online with no fees?

You can currently pay rent online using an e-check for zero fees. You can do this in the tenant portal.

### Why has my maintenance request not been completed yet?

Maintenance requests often require owner approval unless they are maintenance emergencies. If the request has not been completed yet, most likely our vendor is awaiting owner approval to perform the work or the vendor has not been able to get a hold of the tenant. Please ensure if you receive a call from a vendor you schedule it with them. If you have not heard from us a week after submitting the request please send us an email at info@triexmanagement.com.

### How do i get my security deposit refunded?

The most common security deposit charges can be avoided by keeping the following things in mind:

-   If you are going to hang things on the wall, do so with paint safe adhesive strips, not nails. Often simple nail holes cause hundreds of dollars of drywall and paint damage.
    
-   Be careful with the shower curtain/door - a little water can cause a lot of damage!
    
-   Routinely change furnace filters - this is required in TriEx Management’s lease.
    
-   Regular household cleanup prevents the buildup of dust and dirt which can become ingrained in carpets and soft surfaces.
    

### How long does it take to receive my security deposit refund?

NRS.118A requires a security deposit check and statement to be postmarked 30 days from the move-out date. TriEx Management does everything it can to process deposits in a timely manner, but it usually takes a week or two for our vendors to give us an invoice or bid for their services which use to charge the security deposit.

### How do i turn in my 30 day notice to vacate?

Click [here](/s/30-Day-NTV) for our 30 Day Notice to Vacate form. After you fill it out, please email it to info@triexmanagement.com or bring it to our office. **You must turn your keys into the office on the move-out date listed on the notice. Failure to turn keys into the office will result in prorated rent charged until keys are turned in.**

### What do I do in case of a Maintenance Emergency?

A maintenance emergency is defined as: fire, flood or leaks, non functioning plumbing, non functioning heating, or non functioning hot water. **_If the emergency results in a life safety hazard, IMMEDIATELY DIAL 911, then call the maintenance emergency line, (775) 391-8088._**
